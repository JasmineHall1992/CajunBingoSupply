Meet Our Team

The heart and soul of Cajun Bingo Supply

Emel & Ruth Collins

Co-Founder & CEO  ·  Director of Operations

Emel brings more than three decades of industry expertise as our founder and strategist, while Ruth ensures every order, delivery, and customer relationship runs smoothly. Together, they built Cajun Bingo Supply into what it is today.

What started as a small, hands-on operation has grown into one of the most trusted names in charitable gaming supply across Louisiana and Mississippi — but Emel and Ruth have never let the business outgrow the personal touch that built it. Customers still get a real conversation, not just an invoice.

Their approach has always been simple: treat every organization's fundraiser like it's their own, and never stop showing up for the communities that made this business possible.

Fun Fact: Ask Emel about the early days of hand-typed invoices around the kitchen table — he loves telling that story.

Joey Collins

Co-Founder & COO

Bingo has been part of Joey's life for as long as he can remember — he grew up around bingo halls and charitable gaming events long before it became his career.

Today, he helps organizations across Louisiana and Mississippi run successful fundraising programs, building lasting relationships with the people and organizations that make charitable gaming meaningful.

Outside the office, you're just as likely to find Joey at a local bingo hall on a Friday night as behind a desk — for him, this has always been less of a job and more of a community he grew up in.

He's especially proud of the long-term partnerships he's built with charities and organizations who now consider Cajun Bingo Supply less of a vendor and more of a teammate in their fundraising success.

Fun Fact: Joey could count pull-tabs before he could count to ten.

Lee Myers

Since joining Cajun Bingo Supply in 2004, Lee has built lasting relationships with customers throughout Southwest Louisiana through dependable service, honest advice, and a genuine commitment to the charities he serves.

Known affectionately as the company's “big teddy bear,” Lee is quick with a joke and always ready to brighten someone's day — his warm personality and steady work ethic have made him a trusted face of the company for more than two decades.

Fun Fact: If you hear laughter in the bingo hall, there's a good chance Lee just walked through the door.

Brent Gunter

Brent plays a key role in managing inventory, coordinating warehouse operations, and ensuring timely deliveries.

A true jack of all trades, his dependability and hands-on approach help keep the business running smoothly behind the scenes.

Whether it's loading a truck before sunrise or tracking down a last-minute order, Brent brings the same steady, get-it-done attitude to everything he touches — customers can count on their orders arriving complete, correctly packed, and on time.

Fun Fact: If something needs to be fixed, organized, delivered, or figured out, chances are Brent is already on it.
