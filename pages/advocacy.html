Advocacy & the Future of Charitable Gaming

Fighting for the future of charitable gaming in Louisiana

At Cajun Bingo Supply, our commitment to our customers extends far beyond the products we sell. Together with our trusted industry partner, Shreveport Bossier Bingo Supply, we are the only two charitable gaming suppliers in Louisiana actively investing in legislative and regulatory advocacy on behalf of our industry and the charities it serves. We aren't simply doing business in charitable gaming. We're fighting for its future.

We maintain open communication with Louisiana's Office of Charitable Gaming and state policymakers, working to ensure the voices and needs of charitable organizations are heard. As technology, fundraising, and competition continue to evolve, we believe Louisiana's laws and regulations must evolve with them. Being proactive and adaptable today helps ensure charities still have successful fundraising opportunities tomorrow.

Our efforts are strengthened by K2 Advocacy Group in Baton Rouge, whose legislative and regulatory expertise provides dedicated support as we address issues affecting charitable gaming throughout Louisiana.

At the heart of it all is a simple belief: without thriving charities, there is no charitable gaming industry. That's why we continue to show up, speak up, and fight for meaningful progress.

We're the ones fighting this fight because our customers and their missions are worth fighting for. Together, we make change happen, creating opportunities for successful fundraising and a stronger future for Louisiana's charities.
