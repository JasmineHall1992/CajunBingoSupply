About Us

Family-owned, Louisiana-proud since 1989

Founded in 1989 by Emel Collins Jr. and his wife, Ruth, Cajun Bingo Supply began the way many family businesses do — around a kitchen table, with a typewriter, a vision, and a commitment to serving charitable organizations.

What started with hand-typed invoices and long hours of hard work grew, over time, into one of Louisiana's most respected charitable gaming distributors — built on service, integrity, and long-term relationships.

More than three decades later, Cajun Bingo Supply remains a true family business. The same hands-on values that built this company still guide how we work with every charitable organization we serve today.

Our mission remains unchanged: to provide charitable organizations with innovative gaming products, dependable service, and the expertise needed to run successful, profitable bingo operations. Have a large order or a specific need? Get in touch — we're happy to help.
